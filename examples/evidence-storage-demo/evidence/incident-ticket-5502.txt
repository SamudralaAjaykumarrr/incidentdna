Ticket: INC-5502
Summary: Users on shard cache-07 reported unexpected logout starting 14:03 UTC.
Reporter: on-call-engineer (fictional)
Notes: Correlated with a scheduled cache-07 node restart. Session validation
fell back to auth-service, which could not absorb the full cache-miss volume
and began shedding load, causing valid sessions to be treated as invalid.
Resolution: Session cache client updated to retry against a replica shard
before falling back to auth-service; auth-service rate limiting adjusted
upward for validation-fallback traffic.
